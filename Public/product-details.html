A Living Legacy

More than a stool — a sacred vessel of memory and power.

Heritage & History

Carved from a single sacred log, the Entebe served as the official seat of authority for kings, clan chiefs, and family heads in the Buganda Kingdom. Its height and detailed carvings were deliberate symbols of rank and respect. No one was permitted to step over it — doing so was seen as a grave cultural offense.

✦ Spirits & Meaning

Deeply spiritual, the Entebe was believed to be the resting place of ancestral guardian spirits (Mizimu). After the death of a chief or elder, the stool was preserved and venerated in the family shrine, serving as a bridge between the living and the ancestors for generations.
