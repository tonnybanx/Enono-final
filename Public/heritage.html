Living Traditions

Spiritual Heritage of Buganda

Discover the great spirits (Balubaale) who shape the spiritual landscape of Buganda — and the sacred objects traditionally used to honour them.

The Living Spirits

In Buganda tradition, the Balubaale are powerful ancestral and natural spirits who continue to influence daily life, protection, prosperity and the passage between worlds. Use the arrows to explore each spirit and the sacred objects associated with them.

Mukasa

Guardian of the Waters

One of the most revered spirits in Buganda, associated with Lake Victoria and all bodies of water. Invoked for prosperity, fertility, safe journeys and protection of the home.
